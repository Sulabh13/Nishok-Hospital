ABOUT US

Balaghat's Trusted Specialist Hospital

Nishok Hospital is a premier multi-specialty medical facility located in the heart of Balaghat, Madhya Pradesh. Established with a clear vision of bringing world-class medical care to the people of Central India, we have grown to become the most trusted name in Orthopedic and Dermatology healthcare across Balaghat, Gondia, Waraseoni, and Seoni.
From complex fracture surgeries to advanced skin treatments, our doctors bring specialist expertise combined with deep compassion — treating every patient with the care and dignity they deserve.

Serving Our Community

Healthcare for Balaghat, Gondia, Waraseoni & Beyond

Nishok Hospital is proud to serve the medical needs of patients not just from Balaghat city, but also from the wider Balaghat district and neighboring regions including Gondia (Maharashtra), Waraseoni, and Seoni. Many patients from rural areas of Madhya Pradesh travel to Nishok Hospital for specialist care they cannot find locally. We understand the trust patients place in us when they choose Nishok Hospital. That trust is the foundation of everything we do — and we work every day to deserve it.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, expert, and compassionate medical care to every patient in Balaghat and surrounding regions — ensuring no one has to travel far for specialist treatment.

Our Vision

To become Central India's leading destination for orthopedic and dermatological healthcare — where every patient walks out healthier, happier, and confident.

Our Values

Integrity · Compassion · Excellence · Innovation · Patient-First Care
