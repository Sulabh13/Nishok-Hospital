Orthopedic Services

Fracture Treatment & Bone Setting

  • Digital X-Ray for accurate fracture assessment
  • Reduction (bone realignment) — surgical or non-surgical
  • Casting, splinting, or surgical fixation

Knee Replacement Surgery

  • Partial or total knee replacement using modern implants
  • Pre-surgical evaluation and imaging
  • Post-operative physiotherapy and rehabilitation

Arthritis Treatment

  • Medication management — anti-inflammatory drugs
  • Physiotherapy and targeted exercises
  • Joint injections for pain relief

Back Pain & Slip Disc Treatment

  • Conservative management — rest, medication, physiotherapy
  • Epidural injections for severe pain
  • Minimally invasive surgical options

Sports Injury Rehabilitation

  • ACL and ligament tear treatment
  • Tendon injuries and stress fractures
  • Recovery plan for return to activity

Dermatology & Skin Services

Acne & Pimple Treatment

  • Skin assessment and acne type identification
  • Customized topical and oral medication
  • Chemical peels and laser treatments

Pigmentation & Melasma Treatment

  • Prescription depigmentation creams
  • Chemical peels for skin brightening
  • Laser pigmentation removal

PRP Hair Loss Treatment

  • Platelet-rich plasma therapy
  • Stimulates hair follicles naturally
  • 3–6 sessions recommended

HydraFacial Skin Treatment

  • Deep cleansing and pore decongestion
  • Instant hydration and skin plumping
  • Improved skin texture, tone, and radiance

Laser Skin Rejuvenation

  • Laser pigmentation removal for dark spots and melasma
  • Laser acne scar treatment
  • Skin resurfacing and rejuvenation
